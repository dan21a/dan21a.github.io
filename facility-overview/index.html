Our Facility

Welcome to The Hub!

Located at the heart of campus on Landis Green, in the Shores building (see map), the Innovation Hub provides 14,000 square feet of space for collaboration: classes, workshops, special events, meetings, hackathons, design sprints, projects, or just hanging out with amazing people. The space supports Design Thinking with abundant white boards, markers, and post-its. Our Digital Fablab, Design Lab, XR Lab, and Electronics Lab support all kinds of innovative projects. Visit The Hub to relax and work independently or to collaborate with your friends. Grab a snack and a drink at Argo Tea while you’re visiting. You can sign up for workshops in all kinds of technical areas. Our friendly staff will show you the ropes and make you feel right at home. Can’t visit in person? Visit our website and learn online with video tutorials. Earn badges, and qualify as an FSU Innovation Scholar!

What’s @ The Hub?

 

Colab and Lounge

Our spacious Colab is perfect for independent or collaborative work. Also ideal for events, with space for up to 100 - or 30 with social distancing. The furnishings can be configured to support all kinds of activities. It’s perfect for Design Thinking with whiteboards, markers and post-its readily available. Large screen TV’s support remote collaboration via Zoom, and Inspiration Lounge is perfect for reflecting and dreaming up your next big invention!

Study and Class Rooms

The Hub features two class rooms which support credit-earning classes during the day, and workshops, hosted by The Hub and our partners, in the evening. We also provide two group study rooms (huddle rooms), and two smaller study rooms (phone booths), that can be reserved by students any time. Unfortunately, we are not supporting student group work until after social-distancing ends.

Design Lab

Our Design Lab provides high-end design computers with software for 3D and 2D design. Students can design their invention, and then send it to our 3D printers for prototyping. Or, develop virtual environment and objects, and experience them on our VR or AR headsets. We also have two Microsoft Surface Studio 2 workstations that function as the uptimate artist’s tablet.

Digital Fablab

Our Digital Fablab is home to over 40 FDM 3D printers to support prototyping and creative projects. It also features a Laser Cutter and Vinyl Cutter. Our expert staff will show you how to get started! Submit print jobs online and use our reservation system to book time with our experts.

VR Lab

The Hub provides students with access to all the most popular VR and AR equipment: Oculus Rift, Oculus Quest, HTC Vive, Microsoft Hololens, and more, along with a wide variety of software to experience. We support the development of VR and AR applications. Never tried VR or AR? Our staff will be happy to get your started!

Robotics and Electronics

Learn to build a robot with VEX, or program a drone to carry out a useful task. We also have Arduino and Raspberry Pi boards and kits to get you started on your first electronics project. Breadboards, capacitors, sensors, buttons, switches, LEDs, and soldering stations for the more advanced.
