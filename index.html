Florida State University
Designing the future of higher education!

Innovation thrives at the liminal boundaries between disciplines. Faculty, staff, and students work together across boundaries to proactively adopt innovative ideas, leverage disruptive technologies, while exploring impactful methods of teaching, learning, and research.

Areas of Focus

  • Innovation Hub

    An interdisciplinary home for innovation, FSU’s Innovation Hub brings together faculty, staff, and students to address the wicked challenges of our day through human creativity, design thinking, and emerging technologies.

    Learn More about the Innovation Hub

  • Student Innovation

    FSU students combine coursework, co-curricular activities, and self-directed learning to craft their own educational experiences, expand their technical skills, work in interdisciplinary teams, and earn a minor in Innovation.

    Learn more about Student Innovation

  • Faculty Innovation

    FSU faculty and staff work together across departments and disciplines to explore disruptive technologies and innovative methodologies, engage in innovative teaching and research, and define the future of higher education.

    Learn more about Faculty Innovation

  • Experiential Learning

    FSU faculty, staff, and students partner with community organizations and industry experts to solve real-world problems, develop interdisciplinary problem-solving skills, while having a positive impact on the world.

    Learn more about Experiential Learning
