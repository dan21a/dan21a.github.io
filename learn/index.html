Learn

Explore, learn, and upskill.

The Innovation Hub offers many opportunities for students to learn new technology skills
through workshops, online tutorials, and studio-style live streaming. Students can also earn digital badges that indicate their
level of proficiency in various technologies and skill sets.

Studio-style Live Streams

In-person workshops are being replaced with live sessions by our talented and skilled student interns and staff members. Drop by and learn as they share tips and “how-to”s on a variety of software and topics.

Learn more

Online Tutorials

Cannot attend a workshop in person? Explore the various online tutorials created by our technology-savvy interns and staff members. The tutorials are project-based or demonstrations.

Learn more

Digital Badges

Develop new skills and earn digital badges. These multi-level badges let your peers and future employers know of your additional skillsets. These badges can be viewed on your online portfolio and LinkedIn profile.

Learn more
