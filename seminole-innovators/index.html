The Seminole Innovators organization is designed to give students the opportunity to collaborate to solve challenging problems, build unique & valuable skills, and explore the world of emerging technology!

At the core of our organization are Seminole Innovators Intro Projects (SIIPs), which are designed for students of all skill levels, with a special emphasis on being approachable for students with zero experience in a given subject area.

Areas include Programming, 3D Design/Printing, Virtual Reality, and more. As a departmental affiliate working closely in tandem with the Innovation Hub, members are able to find their home on campus and open new doors to innovation & problem-solving. Get involved with us!

SPRING 2025 PROJECTS

  • OurPatch aims to support the Tallahassee community to connect & act more sustainably. This will be achieved through a web application, including an interactive map and a social feed page. The map will highlight local events like trash clean-ups, invasive plant pulls, or even just birdwatching. These events can be shared by users or local organizations. Recycle points and nature areas will also be highlighted. The social side of the app will allow users to interact with their community by sharing nature finds or events they went to.

    • Web Development (Frontend & Backend)

    • UX/UI Design

    • Databases

    • Web Scraping

    • Graphic Design

  • ParkSense aims to revolutionize the parking experience for people with disabilities on campus. By providing important accessibility information, as well as AR navigation, we aim to significantly reduce the time and frustration associated with finding & choosing disabled parking spots at FSU. This will be achieved through a Mobile App (iOS and Android).

  • GameIQ aims to be an advanced sports analytics platform that combines historical data analysis with real-time simulation to enhance decision-making for coaches, analysts, and fans. The tool will provide two core functionalities: a Strategic Play Analyzer & a Real-Time "What If?" Simulator. Combining the fundamentals of LLMs, GenAI & data science, GameIQ is a first of its kind at Seminole Innovators.

Project Archive

  • Art Vending Machine

    Project Lead: Rob Duarte, Professor of Art @ FSU

    In special collaboration with the Thomasville Center for the Arts in Thomasville, Georgia, FSU Professor Rob Duarte & TCFTA Exhibitions Director Samantha McCoy joined forces to unite students across majors at FSU to create a unique blend of art & engineering: the Art Vending Machine.

    Meant to serve as a permanent installation in the Center, the Vending Machine was re-engineered from the ground up to dispense art from local artists.

    The original machine, a 1985 U-SELECT-IT™ Corp. LANCE DROP SHELF VENDOR., dispensed chips/crackers. With the new life given to the machine by the group of dedicated students, the machine is poised to provide a unique & memorable experience for customers for years to come!

    See More

  • Seminole Innovators Try-N-Escape (S.I.T.N.E)

    Project Lead: Jacob Javor, Innovation Hub Special OPS

    This was the first official Seminole Innovators Intro Project (SIIP)! Utilizing the Innovation Hub’s Gail Rubini New Media Design Lab, we assembled a team of undergraduate & graduate students to create a Virtual Reality Escape Room experience.

    Each level is connected via a Magic Warp Elevator™ that must be powered to progress. To Escape, players must make their way through a series of unique, physics-defying levels to solve a variety of puzzles and collect the Elevator Battery.

    This team was made up almost entirely of first-time programmers/developers, artists, and sound designers. Levels include a witch’s lair, a space laboratory, a music room haunted by a ghostly pianist, a time travel room, and–of course–a hamster cage.
